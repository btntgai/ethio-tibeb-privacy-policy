1. Introduction

Ethio Tibeb is a digital entertainment, media, and business discovery platform developed and published by Binyam Tekalign, an independent application developer based in Ethiopia.

Ethio Tibeb provides access to television channels, radio stations, live broadcasts, movies, dramas, music, podcasts, news, educational videos, and other digital media content.

The platform may also provide features for discovering businesses, managing favorite content, accessing personalized recommendations, and exploring media-related services.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ethio Tibeb ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when users access our mobile applications, website, and associated services.

We encourage users to read this policy to understand our privacy practices and available choices.

When users create an account or sign in, we may collect:
